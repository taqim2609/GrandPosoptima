## Planora Planning Documents

Planora manages product lifecycle in [.planora/](.planora/) at the project root.
It contains `README.md`, `manifest.json`, `PRD/`, `UI/`, `DX/`, and `EXECUTION/TODO.md`.

Keep these documents in Git.
Use `$planora-status` to inspect progress, `$planora-next` to execute one item, and `$planora-change` to revise the plan.
